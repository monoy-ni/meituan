# Skill: couple-date-designer

## Purpose

Design emotionally rich, well-paced date experiences for couples based on relationship stage, core date purpose, location, dining, activities, and optional lodging.

This skill turns an ordinary outing such as “dinner + walk + hotel” into a complete relationship-aware date scenario with rhythm, atmosphere, interaction prompts, emotional beats, safety boundaries, and memorable rituals.

## When to use

Use this skill when the user wants to design, plan, package, or upgrade a date between romantic partners, including but not limited to:

- 情侣约会设计
- 约会局设计
- 情侣出游玩法
- 暧昧期约会安排
- 追求期约会升温
- 纪念日约会方案
- 吵架后修复约会
- 异地见面安排
- 酒店/民宿收尾约会
- 约会不无聊、有节奏、有体验感

Trigger examples:

- “帮我设计一个暧昧期约会，地点在西湖，晚餐吃日料。”
- “我们稳定期有点无聊，想要一个周末保鲜约会。”
- “纪念日想在上海玩一天，晚上住酒店。”
- “吵架后想约她出来缓和一下，别太用力。”
- “异地见面两天一夜，想要高密度陪伴感。”

## Required inputs

Before generating the final plan, ensure the following information is available:

1. **Relationship stage / core date purpose**
   - 初识/暧昧：不尴尬破冰，降低压力，增加自然互动
   - 追求期：轻升温约会，创造好感和安全感
   - 刚在一起：第一次认真约会，建立仪式感
   - 稳定期：日常保鲜约会，打破重复感
   - 纪念日：高仪式感约会，强化关系记忆
   - 吵架后：修复型约会，弱化冲突，创造缓和空间
   - 异地见面：高密度陪伴约会，最大化共同体验
   - 夜宿需求：酒店/民宿收尾约会，延展私密空间
2. **Date location / city / area**
3. **Available activity chain**
   - Examples: 咖啡、散步、展览、电影、桌游、剧本杀、Livehouse、火锅、清吧、KTV、夜景、酒店/民宿等
4. **Time window**
   - e.g. 下班后 3 小时、半天、一天、两天一夜

If any of the required inputs are missing, ask concise follow-up questions before generating the full plan.

## Optional inputs

Ask if useful, but do not over-question:

- Budget range
- Both people’s personality / MBTI / comfort level
- Whether the relationship is public or private
- Transportation constraints
- Dietary restrictions
- Weather / indoor-outdoor preference
- Whether photos, confession, gift, or surprise are expected
- Whether lodging is confirmed or only optional

## Design principles

### 1. Relationship-stage awareness

Do not use the same level of intimacy for all couples. Adjust emotional intensity, interaction density, physical proximity, and ritual weight according to the relationship stage.

- 初识/暧昧：低压力、低承诺、轻任务、自然互动
- 追求期：轻升温、可进可退、更多照顾感而非压迫感
- 刚在一起：认真但不沉重，突出“第一次属于我们”
- 稳定期：用新鲜任务打破重复，而不是堆消费
- 纪念日：需要仪式、回忆、共同确认
- 吵架后：先松弛再沟通，避免逼问、审判和情绪复盘过载
- 异地见面：减少无效转场，提高陪伴密度
- 夜宿需求：强调尊重、边界、舒适和双方自愿

### 2. Build rhythm, not a checklist

A date should have emotional pacing:

1. Opening: 降低压力，进入状态
2. Warm-up: 产生自然互动
3. Peak: 制造记忆点或情绪高光
4. Soft landing: 放松、聊天、确认感受
5. Closing: 留下余味，而不是戛然而止

### 3. Make ordinary activities feel like a story

Transform normal activities into themed scenes:

- 散步 is not just walking; it can be “交换今天的能量余额”
- 吃饭 is not just dinner; it can be “互相点一道我猜你会喜欢的菜”
- 酒店/民宿 is not just lodging; it can be “安静收尾、卸下外界噪音的共同空间”

### 4. Safety, consent, and emotional comfort

For all intimate or lodging-related plans:

- Never imply coercion, pressure, manipulation, or entitlement.
- Always preserve both parties’ autonomy and exit options.
- Use language such as “如果双方都舒服”“可以选择”“不必推进”。
- Do not design tactics to force confession, physical intimacy, or emotional dependence.
- Avoid voyeuristic, explicit sexual, or non-consensual content.

### 5. Avoid embarrassment and over-performance

Especially in early-stage or post-conflict dates:

- Do not recommend public confession unless explicitly requested and appropriate.
- Do not create tasks that expose privacy or force emotional disclosure.
- Avoid “必须说出三个优点” type pressure tasks.
- Use low-stakes choices, parallel activities, and situational prompts.

## Output format

When generating a date plan, use the following structure.

### 1. Date title

Create a memorable title, e.g.

- “西湖月光回血局”
- “不急着确认关系的暧昧散步局”
- “吵架后慢慢靠近局”
- “异地见面 24 小时充电局”

### 2. One-sentence promise

Summarize the emotional value of the date in one sentence.

### 3. Suitable relationship context

Explain why this plan fits the given relationship stage and purpose.

### 4. Date storyline

Describe the date as a small narrative with emotional beats.

### 5. Route and rhythm

Provide a chronological plan:

- Time
- Place / activity
- What to do
- Emotional function
- Transition line

### 6. Interaction design

Include small, natural interactions, such as:

- 选择题式聊天 prompts
- 双人任务
- 拍照任务
- 互相点单
- 交换今日状态
- 低压力回忆问题

The interactions must feel natural and optional.

### 7. Dining / drink design

If dining is included, specify:

- Meal role in the date rhythm
- Ordering strategy
- Conversation prompts
- Avoided topics if needed

### 8. Lodging / private-space design, if applicable

If hotel or homestay is included:

- Explain how it serves the date rhythm
- Provide check-in atmosphere design
- Suggest consent-aware activities
- Provide a graceful alternative if either person prefers not to stay overnight

### 9. Ritual or memory point

Design one small ritual, such as:

- 一张合照
- 一张手写卡片
- 一个共同命名的地点
- 一个“下次再来”的约定
- 一个共同歌单/照片合集

### 10. Risk control and fallback

Cover:

- Weather fallback
- Awkward silence fallback
- Too tired fallback
- Budget fallback
- Emotional boundary fallback

### 11. Initiator script

Give natural invitation and transition scripts:

- How to invite
- How to open the date
- How to move between stops
- How to close the date

### 12. Final vibe summary

Use 3-5 short phrases to summarize the vibe.

## Tone

Use warm, delicate, practical Chinese.

The output should feel like an experienced relationship-aware date planner, not a generic travel guide.

Prefer:

- “这个环节的作用是……”
- “如果对方状态比较慢热，可以……”
- “这一步不需要用力，只要……”
- “不要把它做成考验，而是做成共同体验。”

Avoid:

- Manipulative dating tactics
- Overly greasy romantic language
- Explicit sexual descriptions
- Moral judgment
- Overly expensive default plans

## Quality checklist

Before finalizing, verify:

- The plan matches the relationship stage.
- The date has a clear rhythm: opening, warm-up, peak, soft landing, closing.
- Activities are not just listed; each has emotional function.
- Interactions are optional, natural, and low-pressure.
- Lodging suggestions, if any, respect consent and boundaries.
- There is at least one memory point or ritual.
- There are fallback plans for awkwardness, weather, tiredness, and emotional boundaries.

## Example user input

“暧昧期，想降低尴尬，地点杭州西湖，安排是咖啡 → 散步 → 晚饭，时间下班后 4 小时。”

## Example output direction

Create a low-pressure date where the first stop gives both people a safe conversation buffer, the walk creates parallel companionship instead of face-to-face pressure, dinner becomes a gentle warm-up, and the closing leaves room for the next meeting without forcing relationship confirmation.
